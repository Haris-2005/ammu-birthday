The memories I couldn't put on a timeline

Some Memories Don't Have a Date ❤️

Not every memory we made has a date written down.
Some photos are missing.
Some dates have slipped away from my memory.
And some moments happened so naturally that I never thought I'd need to remember the exact day.

But that doesn't make those moments any less important.

I may forget the date, but I'll never forget the feeling.

This timeline only shows the moments I managed to capture — but our story is much bigger than these dates and photographs.

There are countless conversations, laughs, little fights, cute moments, random rides, quiet silences, and memories that exist only in our minds.

So if you ever look through this and think, “Where is that day?”...

Just remember:
I didn't forget you.
I only forgot the date. ❤️

And maybe that's okay.
Because our story was never about keeping a perfect record.
It was about living it.

En Ammu-ku ❤️

Ammu,

Unna enna nu explain panrathuke enakku sometimes words eh kedaikadhu. Nee enakku everything.

Naan unakku best person nu solla maaten, because naan perfect illa. En kitta neraya mistakes irukku, flaws irukku, innum neraya things naan improve pannanum.

Aana oru vishayam mattum naan promise panren... unakkaaga better aaga try panradha naan eppavume stop panna maaten.

Innaiku naan best ah irukkaama irukkalam. Aana everyday konjam konjama better aaga try pannitu iruppen. Unakku best ah irukka mudinja varaikum naan try pannitu iruppen.

Nee enakku girlfriend mattum illa. Nee en love, en best friend, en comfort, en happiness, en person. En heart-la nee enakku family maari aayita. En amma, en appa, en brother, en friend... ellame nee.

Morning kanna thorandha odane vara first thought nee. Night thoonga pora time-la vara last thought-um nee.

Yaaravathu enna, “Kanna mooditu unakku romba important ah irukkura oru person-a think pannu” nu sonna... naan yosikka kooda theva illa. En mind-la immediately varradhu nee dhaan. Every single time.

Eppadi nee en life-la ivlo important aayita nu enakkum sometimes puriyadhu. Aana oru point-ku apram, enoda every happiness-la, every thought-la, every memory-la nee oru part aayita.

Adhaan naan unakkaaga indha website create panninen. Idhu just oru birthday gift illa. Idhu namma story.

Namma dates. Namma photos. Namma cute moments. Namma sirippu. Namma sanda. Namma overcome panna moments. Date kooda nyabagam illaadha memories kooda.

Date-a naan marandhirukkalam... aana andha moment-la naan feel pannadhu mattum marakka maaten.

I love you, Ammu. ❤️

En life-la nee irukkuradhu enakku romba periya blessing. And whatever happens, naan unakkaaga better aaga try panradha mattum eppavume stop panna maaten.

Happy Birthday, en Ammu. ❤️

Namma journey

Namma Journey ❤️

Ammu,

Namma story starting-la irundhu ippo varaikum yosicha... idhu just happy moments mattum illa.

Namma kulla neraya beautiful moments irundhurukku. Aana adhe time-la, neraya struggles, misunderstandings, fights, tears, difficult situations-um irundhurukku.

Sila times-la namakku rendu perukkum romba kashtama irundhurukku. Sila moments-la enna nadakkum nu kooda theriyama irundhurukku.

Especially namma overcome panna try pannina andha huge problems... adhu easy illa.

Aana andha difficult moments ellam irundhalum, namma story-la irundha oru vishayam enakku romba important — namma rendu perum create panna memories.

15 March-la best friends-ah meet pannadhu... 13 April-la namma relationship start aana day... 20 March 2026-la namma little park moment... June-la namma first date... July-la first coffee... August-la UNO, panda keychain, andha first kiss... September-la un birthday... bus rides... bike rides... movie dates... food street... museum... unakku naan feed pannadhu... namma rendu perum serndhu study pannadhu... nee en mela comfortable-ah ukkandhu irundha andha little moments...

Ippadi paatha, namma story-la oru single big moment mattum illa. Hundreds of small moments serndhu dhaan namma story aayirukku.

Sometimes naan think pannuven... namma photos-la irukkura smiles mattum dhaan namma story nu illa.

Photos edukka mudiyadha moments-um irukku. Date nyabagam illaadha moments-um irukku. Camera illadha moments-um irukku. Aana en mind-la save aana memories neraya irukku.

Andha memories-ku timestamp illa... aana meaning irukku.

Namma perfect couple illa. Naan perfect boyfriend-um illa. Nee perfect girlfriend-a irukkanum nu naan expect pannavum illa.

But I want us to keep learning. Keep understanding. Keep growing. And most importantly... keep choosing each other.

Because enakku namma story-la perfect days mattum venum nu illa. Kashtama irukkura days-la kooda, rendu perum konjam konjama better-a understand pannitu, one day at a time move forward panna mudinja... adhu dhaan enakku important.

Namma story innum mudinjidala. Idhu just oru chapter dhaan. ❤️
